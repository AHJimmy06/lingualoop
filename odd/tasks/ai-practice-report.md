# AI-drafted practice report

## Objective and authority
The user explicitly requests the complete practice report be drafted by AI rather than only assembling turn-level AI evidence deterministically. They selected **new reports only**: historical saved deterministic reports remain unchanged; no retroactive inference or upgrade button. Preserve accurate `generatedWithAi` provenance. Existing single-session owner authorization, closed-session admission, exact turn evidence and early Finish semantics remain intact.

## Constraints and defaults
- Do not contact unrelated port 8080, NousAI or other services. Never print credentials, copy secret files or run live paid/provider requests during implementation or tests. Use injected fake Responses clients. No push. Preserve unrelated dirty files including the existing `$4::integer` session-store change.
- Existing reports replay immutably and for free. New reports with completed evidence require one bounded OpenAI drafting call, constrained to the configured practice model/key, owner/session scope, bounded input/output and strict schema. Fail closed without persisting a false 'AI' claim when unavailable or malformed; a later request may retry. For a closed session with zero completed turns, retain the honest deterministic no-evidence report with `generatedWithAi:false`; AI cannot assess missing evidence.
- Do not hold a PostgreSQL row lock across a slow external call. Design a durable claim/lease or another bounded mechanism that avoids duplicate paid calls from concurrent requests and safely handles failure/retry; do not promise exactly once if the provider outcome is uncertain. Keep existing reports and responses backward-compatible.
- No automatic paid smoke or authenticated turn. Local deployment only after focused/full tests and an explicit safe operational decision; no push.
